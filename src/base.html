Fretopia

Constellation line art, playable instruments and fluid gestures, before they go into the app.

  1. Landing: strum down or up across the strings. Soft or fast, near the middle or the edge: each string answers differently. Push slowly to bend one, rest on it to mute it.
  2. Begin opens the picker. Drag a card by its name, or use ← →, to swipe.
  3. Play the card in front: strum the guitar, sitar or tanpura, press harmonium keys, tap the tabla (hold the left drum and slide), and touch a bansuri hole and slide for meend.
Sound starts after your first click or tap; on iPhone, also switch off silent mode. Guitar sound: real acoustic guitar recordings (University of Iowa samples via tonejs-instruments, CC BY 3.0). Guitar art: your line drawing.
